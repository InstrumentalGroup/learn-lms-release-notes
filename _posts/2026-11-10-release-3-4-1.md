## Theme modules

**Course content (versions 1 and 2)**

- New support for **Livid** videos as a lesson video source.
- Like YouTube and HubSpot videos, the **Next lesson** button stays locked until a Livid video has been watched.

## Courses and HubSpot sync

- Subscription management: new back-end support for managing subscriptions.

## Fixes

- None.

## Action required

- Download the latest release of the Learn LMS theme and upload it to your portal.
- **Only if you use a child theme or edited the theme code:** the Livid video support changed these files. Compare them with your copy and apply the changes:
  - `macros/_lms_next_chapter_macro.html` and `macros/_lms_next_chapter_macro_v2.html`: `livid_video` was added to the video check that locks the **Next lesson** button until the video is watched.
  - `modules/lms_course_content.module` and `modules/lms_course_content_v2.module`: `fields.json`, `module.html` and `module.js` (new Livid video option, player and tracking).

  [Download the diff of these changes]({{ site.baseurl }}/downloads/release-3-4-1-theme-changes.patch). It is a guide for porting the changes, not a patch to apply as is.
