## Theme modules

**Course content (versions 1 and 2)**

- Quiz hints are now shown only for questions the learner answered incorrectly. Hints no longer appear next to correct answers, on the results screen after a quiz or when a learner reviews a past attempt.
- Hints now follow the quiz's **Display hint when the answers are wrong** setting as it is configured in the editor. If the box is unchecked, no hints are shown.
- For open questions, a hint is hidden when the learner entered an answer. After an instructor reviews an open question, the hint appears only if the answer was marked incorrect.
- There are no new module settings.

## Course pages and certificates

- Quizzes with hints turned on now show hints only for wrong answers, so learners see help where they need it.

## Fixes

- Hints were shown for every question that had one, including correct answers. They now appear only for wrong answers.
- Hints could appear even when **Display hint when the answers are wrong** was turned off for the quiz. They now respect the setting.

## Action required

- None.
