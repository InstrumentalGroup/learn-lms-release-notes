## Theme modules

**Course content (versions 1 and 2)**

- New setting: **Require students to watch the whole video**. It is a checkbox, and it is unchecked by default.
- When unchecked, learners can skip ahead, and a video or audio lesson completes as soon as they reach the end. The **Next** or **Finish** button then unlocks.
- When checked, a lesson completes only after the learner has actually played about 95% of it. Skipped sections must be watched before the next lesson unlocks. This is the behavior introduced in 3.5.0.
- The setting applies to all YouTube, Livid and HubSpot video lessons and to audio lessons in that module. Pausing, rewinding and resuming later are always allowed.

## Course pages and certificates

- Existing courses no longer require full viewing after you update to this release. To keep requiring it, turn on **Require students to watch the whole video** in the Course content module of each course.

## Action required

- Download the latest release of the Learn LMS theme and upload it to your portal.
- **Only if you want learners to watch videos in full:** open each course page in the HubSpot editor, select the **Course content** module, check **Require students to watch the whole video** and publish.
- **Only if you use a child theme or edited the theme code:** compare your copy of the **Course content** modules (versions 1 and 2) with the new release and apply the changes. They cover the new setting and the video and audio completion rules.
- Learners with a lesson page already open will need to reload it to get the new behavior.
