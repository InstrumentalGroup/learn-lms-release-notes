## Theme modules

**Course page (version 2)**

- New **course details** side panel.

**Course content (versions 1 and 2)**

- Lessons are locked for learners who are not enrolled.
- Videos and other media stop playing when the learner switches to another lesson.
- Slide, PowerPoint and PDF embeds load only when their lesson is shown. Pages with many lessons load faster and no longer show blank or broken slides.

**Quiz**

- The score is shown as a percentage together with the pass mark.
- The downloaded certificate file is named after the course.

**Main menu**

- New optional setting **Highlight the current section** (off by default). It marks the page you are on and its top-level section. Pages that aren't in the menu highlight the closest matching section.

**Login and logout**

- **Logout** now ends the HubSpot membership session and clears the Learn cookies, so learners are no longer signed straight back in. It doesn't end a session at an external single sign-on provider.

## Action required

- None.
