## Theme modules

**Course content (version 2)**

- New lesson setting **Lesson buttons position**, with the options **Course default (Styles tab)**, **Top** and **Bottom**. It sets where the **Mark lesson as complete** and **Next lesson** buttons appear in that lesson: at the very top (before the media and content) or at the very bottom (after both).
- **Course default** uses the existing **Lesson buttons** setting in the **Styles** tab. Lessons saved before this setting existed also use the course default, so existing courses look the same as before.
- **Lesson buttons position** is independent of **Lesson content position**, so you can combine the buttons, media and content in any order.
- **Finish course** always stays at the bottom of the lesson.
- The help text of **Lesson content position** said it also moved the buttons. It now explains that it only moves the lesson content and points to **Lesson buttons position** for the buttons.
- In the HubSpot editor, the **Next lesson** button is now shown, so you can check its custom label and position while editing. It doesn't do anything in the editor. Learners and preview still work as before: **Next** appears once the lesson is marked as complete.

## Fixes

- The custom label of the **Next lesson** button could not be seen while editing a course in the HubSpot editor, because the button was hidden. It is now visible.
- The help text of **Lesson content position** described the wrong behavior. It is corrected.

## Action required

- Download the latest release of the Learn LMS theme and upload it to your portal.
- **Only if you use a child theme or edited the theme code:** compare your copy of the **Course content** module (version 2) with the new release and apply the changes. They cover the new **Lesson buttons position** setting and showing the **Next lesson** button in the editor.
