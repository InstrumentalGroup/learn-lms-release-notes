## Theme modules

**Course content (versions 1 and 2)**

- Video and audio lessons are now completed by the time a learner actually plays, not by reaching the end. This applies to YouTube, Livid and HubSpot videos and to audio lessons.
- A lesson counts as complete once the learner has played at least 95% of it. Skipping ahead leaves the skipped section unwatched, so the lesson stays incomplete and the **Next** or **Finish** button stays locked.
- Watching a section again does not add to the total. Pausing, rewinding and reviewing are not restricted.
- Progress is saved for each learner. If they stop and come back later, they only need to watch the part they haven't seen yet.
- The **Next** and **Finish** buttons unlock only after the lesson is confirmed as complete.
- In the HubSpot editor and in preview there is no learner enrollment, so nothing changes there. Reaching the end of a video, for example by dragging to the last frame, still unlocks **Next** so you can test your course.
- There are no new module settings.

## Course pages and certificates

- Learners can no longer finish a course while a video or audio lesson they have not fully watched is still open. Lessons they had already completed are not affected, so existing progress is not locked.
- Course migration from version 1 to version 2 now works for course pages that use a custom template name. The new page uses the standard version 2 template unless another one is chosen.

## Fixes

- Course changes made in HubSpot could overwrite recent changes made in Learn LMS that hadn't synced yet. They no longer do.
- After uploading a SCORM package, the course could keep linking to the old course page a few seconds later. It now keeps the new page.
- SCORM lessons now save the final completion and bookmark when a learner closes the tab, so completion is no longer lost.
- SCORM progress saved at almost the same time could overwrite newer progress, such as a lower score replacing a higher one, or create duplicate enrollments. Progress now only moves forward.
- Uploading SCORM packages to a second course in the same portal could fail on some accounts. This is corrected.

## Action required

- Download the latest release of the Learn LMS theme and upload it to your portal.
- **Only if you use a child theme or edited the theme code:** compare your copy of the **Course content** modules (versions 1 and 2) and the **Next lesson** button code with the new release and apply the changes. They cover the video tracking, the video and audio completion rules, and the editor and preview behavior.
- Learners with a lesson page already open will need to reload it before their video progress is recorded.
