## Theme modules

**Course content (versions 1 and 2)**

- Watch time is now tracked for YouTube videos, HubSpot videos and audio lessons. Only time spent actually playing counts, so skipping ahead adds nothing. Progress is saved while a learner watches, when they pause, and when they leave the page.
- When a video or audio lesson plays to the end, the lesson is marked as complete and the **Next** or **Finish** button appears. Learners no longer need to click **Mark lesson as complete** after watching.
- A video that was watched to the end stays marked as watched after the page is reloaded, so the **Next** button stays available.
- There are no new module settings.

## Course pages and certificates

- Enrollments now store how many seconds each learner has watched in each video or audio lesson. This is not yet shown in reports.
- Finishing a video counts toward the learner's course progress straight away.

## Fixes

- When the same YouTube video was used in more than one lesson, finishing it in one lesson could mark the video as watched in the others. Only the lesson that was actually watched is now marked.

## Action required

- None.
