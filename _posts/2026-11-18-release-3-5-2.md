## Theme modules

**Course content (versions 1 and 2)**

- When a learner clicks the **Try again** button after a quiz, the quiz now starts over: every previous answer is cleared and the quiz goes back to the first question. This also applies to the retry button shown after passing a quiz, when retries are enabled.
- There are no new module settings.

## Course pages and certificates

- If a SCORM course can't be launched, for example because the course is disabled, the learner isn't registered in the portal, or storage isn't set up, the course page and the HubSpot editor now show a short message. Before, they showed a technical error text.

## Fixes

- After failing a quiz and clicking **Try again**, the earlier answers were still selected. A second retry could also stay on the last question instead of returning to the first. Both are corrected.

## Action required

- Download the latest release of the Learn LMS theme and upload it to your portal.
- **Only if you use a child theme or edited the theme code:** the quiz retry fix changed these files. Compare them with your copy and apply the changes:
  - `macros/_lms_quiz_macro.html` and `macros/_lms_quiz_macro_v2.html`: the **Try again** and retry buttons now reset the quiz form and re-trigger the return to the first question.

  [Download the diff of these changes]({{ site.baseurl }}/downloads/release-3-5-2-theme-changes.patch). It is a guide for porting the changes, not a patch to apply as is.
